Phases from CIF

Multi-phase best fit %
Searches combinations of the candidate phases (the “in” choice next to ★ Best fit: all folders, ★ Starred or one folder) for the set that explains the most observed peaks, with one common calibration factor s. Score = intensity-weighted share of peaks explained, corrected for chance matches, minus a penalty for strong calculated lines (≥ 30 %) that are not observed and a small penalty per extra phase. Every phase in a combination must explain at least one peak the others do not. Click a result to show it (ticks those phases and sets s).

Drop or add any number of .cif files, or whole folders: each folder gets its own box with the folder name. Click a phase to highlight it (shown as a preview with its rings and sticks) and use ↑ ↓ to step through the phases; tick it (or press space) to keep it shown. Calculated with kinematic electron scattering factors; ring intensity ∝ m·|F|²·d². dobs = s × dcalc: s corrects a small calibration error and applies to all phases.
